Frequently asked questions

Does it cost anything to request quotes?

No. Posting a job and receiving quotes is completely free and without obligation. Companies pay a fee to access job requests.

How many quotes will I get?

You will receive up to 5 quotes from companies in your area. Most requests receive their first quote within 24 hours.

Do I have to hire one of the companies?

No. You are free to decline all quotes. You only hire when you have found the right company at the right price.

How are companies checked?

We verify company registration, tax status and credit history, and continuously monitor customer reviews. Companies that don't meet our standards are removed.

Can I get a tax deduction for the work?

In many countries, labour costs for home repairs and renovation are partly tax-deductible. Ask the companies to include the deduction in their quotes.
